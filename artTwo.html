PANDA DRAWING

Step One:

Begin by sketching three circles to outline the Panda's head, ears, body, and paws: a tiny one on the right, a larger one closer to the smaller one, and a medium one further away. Connect them and add a few extra details as shown.

Step Two:

Pay careful attention towards the muzzle. Divide the large circle into four sections and draw two small dots for eyes. Form the mouth and nose from the little circle. Pass the hair along the contour in tiny strokes.

Step Three:

Then, stroke the front and back paws.

Step Four:

In a similar manner, use the eraser to sketch the complete body and delete any unneeded lines.

Step Five:

The colour of the Panda is one of its distinguishing characteristics. Make the ears, front paws, and hind legs darker. Don't forget about the spots around the eyes. Make use of shadows.
