TISSUE PAPER PORTRAIT

Step One:

Lightly sketch an oval head using a pencil. Then create a neck and shoulders underneath that.

Step Two:

Make a profile face with profile features on the left. Include a hairline

Step Three:

Finish with a forward-looking eye on the right side of the face. Add hair and use a marker to trace all of the lines.

Step Five:

Water should be brushed over the drawing. Cut and tear pieces of the bleeding tissue paper, then apply them to the face in the manner depicted. Allow to dry, To flatten any curved edges, take off the tissue paper and press it beneath heavy books.
